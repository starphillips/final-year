#### Participant Information

Participant information should describe your research topic and should also identify the principal investigator. For example:

Example:

Welcome to the research study

We are interested in understanding [TOPIC]. For this study, you will be presented with information relevant to [TOPIC]. Then, you will be asked to answer some questions about it. Your responses will be kept completely confidential.

The study should take you around 30 mins to complete. Your participation in this research is voluntary. You have the right to withdraw at any point during the study. The Principal Investigator of this study can be contacted at [EMAIL].
