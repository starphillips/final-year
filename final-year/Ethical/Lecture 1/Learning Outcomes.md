### Ethical Lens
- Rights Lens - protects and respects the morals of those who are affected
- Justice Lens - fair and equal treatment
- Utilitarian Lens - results based and consequences of actions
- Common Good Lens - what benefits the community
- Virtue Lens - what kind of person am I if I do this?
- Care Lens - rooted in relationships and placing their interests above your own
