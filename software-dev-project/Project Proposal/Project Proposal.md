
Project Title

“A map-based backpacking route generator: grounding route planning in verified data, piloted in Colombia”.

Problem Statement

independent travellers struggle to plan coherent multi-stop routes. Existing tools are limited to single cities, cap trip length, or produce incoherent routes, and LLMs are unreliable at multi-constraint planning. Add two or three failures from your own competitor testing.

Planned Research

a literature review (travel planning, LLM reliability), competitor evaluation using fixed scenarios and error categories, a survey of backpackers, and a comparative user study of your tool against ChatGPT. Mention the ethics checklist and say that Colombia was chosen as the pilot for the reasons above.

Planned Deliverables

a deployed PWA with structured input, a route engine, map view and AI assistant; a verified dataset of 30 to 40 Colombian destinations; evaluation results; the report, video and poster.
