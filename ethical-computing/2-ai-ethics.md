#### Algorithm Bias
Any machine in the world is a product of humans which have biases that we reflect into them.

Stems from:
- What trains the models
- Tools and equipment to collect and analyse data
- Factors and selection criteria - sampling bias
- Changes in wider society. 

Basically - AI tools can yield these biases. AI is not "neutral".

#### Case Study

Ghost plates - reflective material to prevent road cameras from seeing the plates.

Shield Font - AI scrappers take people's intellectual property as its available on the internet. This tool replaces code level work with gibberish making it hard for the AI to scrap the data, . or will deem it as 'non sense', whilst keeping it readable to humans or the UI. Limitations of this: visually impaired need OCR, which will then just read the gibberish, or copy and paste functionality will not work.
