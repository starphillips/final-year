
In your poster, you must:
•	Analyse the case study and identify the range of ethical, professional, and legal issues it raises
•	Select one concern that you judge to be the most significant and justify it
•	Apply an appropriate framework or tool, such as those introduced within the module, to structure and strengthen your argument. 
•	Propose a mitigation for that concern, supported by relevant academic literature.
•	Include a reference list. This may be submitted as a separate document.

Your poster must be:
•	Legible. Consider the balance of text and informative images. There is no specific word count, but text should be reasonably readable.
•	Prepared for a general professional audience. It should not be a marketing poster.
•	Text and images should be correctly cited and referenced according to UWE referencing guidelines.

In the 10 minute Q&A, you will be asked questions about:
•	Your poster, including but not limited to its design, structure, content, or references;
•	Your choice and application of your chosen framework or tool;
•	Any other relevant question related to the assessment.
•	It is your chance to add to the poster and address any inadequacies or limitations.
•	You do not have to prepare a presentation.

Your poster should be your own words and you will need to summarise and use bullet points. If you do want to copy any text direct from another source, make sure you use quotation marks and cite it consistent with UWE referencing guidelines.
